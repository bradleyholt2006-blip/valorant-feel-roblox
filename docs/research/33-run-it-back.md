# System 33: Run it Back

Phoenix's ultimate is Run it Back, on X: with its points full, X marks where he stands, and for the
next 10 s a hit that would kill him doesn't. His body goes instead, and he rises again at the mark
with full health and the shield he had as he cast it. If nothing kills him in those 10 s, his body
goes all the same as they run out, and he comes back the same way. This system adds the cast and its
mark, its time, a hit that would kill him sparing him, his body going and his rising at the mark,
the reload he comes back with, and how all of it is drawn and heard. It also adds what out of play
means to everything else on the server. With it, all of Phoenix's kit plays.

As before, everything comes from public sources: five of Riot's patch notes and, for the numbers
Riot doesn't give (its time, its activation and how long he takes to come back), the VALORANT Wiki's
pages on Run it Back and Phoenix. Liquipedia, a NeuralBoost guide, guides from Player Assist,
esports.net, dodge.gg and EarlyGame, and the title of a Z League article cross-check them. No game
files, memory or network traffic were used. The wiki marks its numbers as read from the game's files
by its editors; here they're the wiki's numbers, labelled community. Nothing in this system is
measured. Status labels:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | Stated by the game's maker (Riot's patch notes) |
| **Community** | Published by a guide, a wiki or the press, not by the maker (the VALORANT Wiki, run by Weird Gloop) |
| **Estimate** | Our own number or reading. It must be measured and tuned before we trust it |

## 1. Observable behaviour

- **The cast** (the wiki and Patch 13.02). "INSTANTLY place a marker at Phoenix's location." It
  lasts "10 seconds (Includes 1-second activation animation)" (from the game's files, there) and
  costs 7 points, 6 before Patch 13.02, which raised it "to address the frequency with which he is
  able to cast his ultimate". The wiki gives its weapon re-equip as "Fast (cast + respawn)".
- **How it ends** (the wiki). "While this ability is active, dying or allowing the timer to expire
  will end this ability". "If Phoenix would be killed during the ability's duration, or after the
  duration expires, Phoenix's body instead dissipates."
- **His return** (the wiki, Patch 5.01 and a guide). It brings "Phoenix back to this location with
  full health and the amount of armor he had when the ability was cast"; Patch 5.01: "Phoenix will
  now spawn at the marker with the amount of shields he had when the ability was cast". "Revive
  duration" "Total 2.4 seconds", a 1-second dissipate and a 1.4-second respawn (from the game's
  files), and "Phoenix returns in the direction he was facing when he initially cast the ability."
  Player Assist's guide: "there will be a small flame on the ground where you will be respawning",
  and "There is a two-second animation when you respawn, where you are vulnerable to attacks and
  cannot move."
- **The reload** (Patch 1.0 and Patch 5.07). "Run it Back automatically reloads all weapons on
  respawn", as "the several second respawn time is enough time to justify an automatic reload".
  Patch 5.07 "Fixed a bug where Phoenix would not automatically re-equip a weapon at the end of Run
  It Back".
- **No kill** (guides, and Patch 1.02). Player Assist's guide: "killing you won't count as a kill";
  a Z League article's title asks "Does Phoenix Ult Count as a Kill in Valorant?", and its address
  answers that it does not. But "Blade Storm now refreshes when used to kill Phoenix during Run It
  Back", and "Soul Orbs are now spawned when Reyna kills Phoenix during Run It Back" (Patch 1.02).
- **The spike** (the wiki's history, Liquipedia and EarlyGame). Patch 1.04 fixed his planting "the
  Spike off the bomb site if he finished planting after Run it Back ended", and 1.05 his
  "auto-defuse the Spike if he was defusing the Spike when Run it Back ended", "by syncing the
  expire time of his ultimate" (Liquipedia). EarlyGame: "Phoenix seems to be the only agent able to
  survive a spike explosion within the radius", reappearing "at the position where you activated
  it".
- **The mark seen** (dodge.gg and esports.net). "enemies who spot the marker location can camp it",
  and "kill Phoenix instantly when he respawns". After his first body's down, abilities from a
  distance can "kill Phoenix for a second time, invalidating Phoenix's ult".
- **Old bugs** (the wiki's history). Patch 0.49: "for a brief time after Run it Back ended, Phoenix
  could be damaged in the place where he fizzled". 12.01: "Fixed a bug where Phoenix would remain
  nearsighted from certain abilities after respawning from Run it Back." 2.01: his "starting
  location would briefly be displayed on the minimap after being killed in Run it Back".

## 5. Design

### The rules

- **`AbilityConfig`** adds `RunItBack`: its duration, its activation, his body's time and his
  rise's, each with its status and source, and our grace. Run it Back is playable.
- **`AbilitySim`** plays it. X with the points full is a `mark` cast from the feet, the way he
  faces. The kit keeps the mark, its yaw, whether he was on the floor and crouched (from
  `PlayerSim`), and `backUntil`, when it runs out. `marking` is the activation, when nothing else is
  cast. `comeBack` is its tick, first in `PlayerSim`'s. As its time runs out it's a `fizzle` cast
  where he is, and `returnAt` is when his body went. Through his return he's held; 1 s on it's a
  `rise` cast at the mark; once he's risen it's over. `fizzle` ends it from the server, for a hit it
  spares. `ultimateOn` keeps its points from being filled while it's on (`fill`, `full`), and
  `summary` says `back`, `away` and `rise`.
- **`MovementSim`**'s `Motion` adds `hold`: a tick with no speed and no fall.
- **`PlayerSim`** holds him through his return. No button reaches the spike, the gun or the
  movement, so a plant or defuse stops; an ability in the fifth slot goes, and a reload, a zoom or a
  burst under way stops. At the `rise` he's at the mark as he stood there, his tag gone, his guns
  reloaded and the gun drawn again, ready as his return ends. The `mark` cast puts the gun away for
  the activation. `knivesBack` is the Blade Storm refill on its own, which `killedWith` uses too.
- **`PlayerCodec`** carries the mark, its yaw, how he stood, `backUntil` and `returnAt`, and
  **`PlayerController`** corrects them as it does the rest of the kit. At the rise it puts the view
  at the mark at once, turned the way he faced and level, and while his return's under way it
  doesn't blend a correction.

### The server

- **`CombatServer`** asks `spares` of a hit that would kill: spared, its victim has taken it, with
  no kill, no kill listeners and no slow. `withdraw` takes an entity out of play with no hit, and
  `back` puts it back with full health and a kept shield.
- **`RunItBackServer`** (new) keeps the shield he has as he casts it, and sets `VFLastMark`. While
  it runs it spares him a hit that would kill him: it ends Run it Back in his simulation from his
  last tick, brings a Blade Storm knife's knives back, and tells every client of his body going (the
  Ability remote's `fizzle`, `spared`). As it runs out it takes him out of play. At his rise he's
  back in play, and a body gone too long on the server's clock is back where it is.
- **`PlayerSimServer`**: out of play, `pose` is nothing, so the snapshots and the rewinds leave him
  out, and `inPlay` says so to the rest. At the `rise` the snapshots start over from the mark.
  **`FlareServer`** blinds and **`GroundGunsServer`**, **`PickupsServer`** and **`SpikeServer`**
  hand things to nobody out of play, and `FlareServer.clear` drops his blind as his body goes.
- **`AbilityServer`** tells every client of the `mark`, the `fizzle` as it runs out, and the `rise`.

### The client

- **`RunItBackView`** (new) draws the mark, a small flame on the floor: yours from your predicted
  kit, others' from the server's `mark` as the frame on your screen reaches it, gone as he rises
  there. Others' bodies going are a burst of flame, and their rising a column of flame that thins
  away. For you, the screen is flame while your body's gone, clearing through your rise. Your body
  going as the server spared you is heard as its word comes.
- **`AbilityHud`** lights X while it runs, with its seconds left, and `BACK` through the return.
- **`AbilityEffects`** hears the cast, the body going and the rise, and **`SoundConfig`** adds them
  (`markLit`, `fizzle`, `markRise`).

### Not here yet

- **Voice lines.** The reference's Phoenix calls out as he casts it and as he comes back; there are
  no voices here.
- **The minimap.** There's none, so there's nothing for 2.01's fix to hide.
- **The kill feed and the points.** No source says whether a hit it spares shows in the reference's
  kill feed or gives its attacker a point; ours shows nothing and gives none.
- **Reyna.** Patch 1.02's Soul Orbs for killing him in it wait for a Reyna.
- **A shield bought after the cast.** It's lost at the rise, as he comes back with the shield he had
  as he cast it (Patch 5.01 read as written).
- **Late word of a hit.** A hit the server spares him reaches his own screen half a round trip
  later, and his client has played on meanwhile: then he's put back where he was hit, his body gone.
  A death is the same.
- **His view.** He comes back turned the way he faced, his view level; no source says where it
  pointed up or down.
- **Late joiners.** Someone who joins while a mark's out doesn't see it, and a mark whose caster
  leaves goes only once its time's up.
- **The look.** The mark, the burst, the column and the screen are ours until clips are stepped
  through.

## 7. Debug tools

- The caster's `VFLastMark` is where they last cast it, their feet, in metres.
- `VFKit` says `back` while it runs, `away` while his body's gone and `rise` as he rises at the
  mark, as `C1/1 Q1/1 E2/2 X0/7 back`.
- `Workspace.VFRunItBack` holds a `Mark` model for each mark drawn, its `Entity` attribute whose it
  is, and the `Burst`, the `Ember`s and the `Rise` while others' bodies go and rise.
- The `VFRunItBack` screen is the flame over your view while your body's gone.
- `VFTestButtons` 32768 presses X. On the course his points fill again 1.5 s after he's back, so it
  can be cast over and over; the course's spike blast spares him as a hit would.

## 8. Refinement loop

1. Step through clips of X pressed with a gun drawn, and set `RunItBack.activation`.
2. Step through clips of Phoenix killed in it, and set `away` and `rise`.
3. Watch it cast crouched and run out in the air, and settle how he stands as he comes back.
4. Kill a Phoenix in it in a custom game, and settle the kill feed and the points.
5. Re-run `luau tests/run.luau`.

## Known gaps to check in Studio

- Run it Back lit on Phoenix's card and the bar, with no `NOT YET`.
- X with its points full: the mark's flame at your feet, the gun away for 1 s, nothing else cast in
  it, and X counting down from 10 on the bar.
- Run out: the screen flame at once, held, then back at the mark facing the way you cast it, held
  1.4 s more as the flame clears, your guns full and the gun in hand as you can move; X `BACK` on
  the bar through it.
- The spike blast in it: no death, no kill feed, and back at the mark with full health and the
  shield you had.
- A second player: the mark's flame seen, the burst as the body goes, the column at the mark, the
  sounds heard; shots through where the body was; a kill in the rise is a kill.
