# System 42: Omen's Shrouded Step

Omen's Shrouded Step is the third of his kit played. C puts it in hand, ready in 0.5 s, with a ring
on the floor where it would take him: along the crosshair, no further than 15 m, and a shadow of him
standing there, both seen through walls and the ring shown on the minimap. A click of fire there
starts its 0.7 s windup, through which he can't move, fire or cast; then he's gone, out of play, and
half a second later he's there, free to move, his gun back in hand 0.7 s after. This system adds
where it takes him, the windup, his time gone and his arrival, the server keeping him out of play
while he's gone, and what everyone sees and hears of it.

As before, everything comes from public sources: four of Riot's patch notes and, for what Riot
doesn't give, the VALORANT Wiki's pages on Shrouded Step and on Team Deathmatch and three guides
(esports.net's, Boosting Ground's and Sportskeeda's). No game files, memory or network traffic were
used, and nothing was read from the live game. Here the wiki's numbers are labelled community,
though it says some of them come from the game files. Riot gives the cost, the windup, where it
takes him shown on the map, an indicator when that point's out of his sight, and that enemies in its
range should hear it; the wiki gives fire starting it, the equip and unequip times, the range, his
time gone and how far his arrival's heard; the guides give that the aim needs a line of sight, that
he can step up onto crates, and a shadow where he's going. Where it takes him when the aim meets a
wall or nothing, the room he needs there, that he must be in sight there, how high it can take him,
what he does through the windup, what it all looks like, and how long the server waits for him are
ours. Nothing in this system is measured. Status labels:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | Stated by the game's maker (Riot's patch notes) |
| **Community** | Published by a guide, a wiki or the press, not by the maker (the VALORANT Wiki, run by Weird Gloop) |
| **Estimate** | Our own number or reading. It must be measured and tuned before we trust it |

No source gives where it takes him when the aim meets a wall, a ceiling or the sky, how much room he
needs there, what the line of sight the guides name runs to, how high it can put him, whether he
keeps falling or sliding through the windup, how the shadow where he's going looks, or what his own
screen shows while he's gone. Ours are in section 4: the floor below where the aim ended, off a wall
by half his width; room for him standing, from a step's height up, drawn back towards him a little
at a time until there is; the line from his eye to his eye there; no floor more than 1 m over his
eye; falling and sliding on as he was; a column of shadow darkening through his time gone; and his
screen dark until he's there.

## 1. Observable behaviour

- **In hand** (the wiki). "EQUIP a shrouded step ability and see its range indicator." It's ready
  0.5 s after C, and the gun is back 0.7 s after he arrives (from the game files, it says). In our
  words, the crosshair is replaced by an indicator of where it would take him.
- **The step** (the wiki and Patch 4.04). "FIRE to begin a brief channel, then teleport to the
  marked location." Patch 4.04 has "Pre teleport delay decreased 1s >>> 0.7s". The wiki has, in our
  words, a range of 15 m (from the game files, it says) and half a second from his going to his
  arrival (its own estimate, tested by hand), 1.2 s in all.
- **Where it takes him** (Patches 1.0 and 4.04, and the guides). Patch 1.0: "Omen can now see his
  teleport location on the map", and he "receives an in-world indicator of where he's targeting when
  his vision of the point is obstructed." Patch 4.04 "Improved the system used to place abilities at
  targeted ground locations.", Shrouded Step among them. esports.net has "You have to have a line of
  sight on the location you want to teleport to." and, in our words, steps up onto crates and a
  tube; Boosting Ground has "15-meter range, provided there is line of sight". The wiki's history,
  in our words: v1.14 and v2.0 stopped it taking him past the buy phase's barriers, v2.02 stopped it
  snapping to corners at long range, and v2.04 stopped it taking him into narrow gaps a Barrier Orb
  made against a wall.
- **Through the windup** (Boosting Ground). He's "unable to shoot or move freely" through it and as
  he arrives; in our words, a cast mid-jump carries him on through the air before he's put where it
  marked.
- **What others see and hear** (the wiki, Boosting Ground, Sportskeeda and Patch 13.00). The wiki
  has, in our words, a sound at his position as it's cast that everyone hears, his growl through it,
  and a smaller sound as he arrives, heard within 12.5 m (from the game files, it says); Boosting
  Ground has only the first, within 12.5 m. Sportskeeda has, in our words, a shadow marker on the
  floor where he's going, with a sound, that opponents aim at, and him sometimes landing "beside the
  shadow marker rather than on it". Patch 13.00: "We’ve updated enemy Omen Shrouded Step audio with
  the goal of improving clarity in situations" "where you are within range of the ability but are
  unable to hear it."
- **Cut short** (the wiki's history, in our words). Since v7.02, KAY/O's ZERO/point or Deadlock's
  Annihilation hitting him in the windup stops it.
- **Its cost** (Patches 3.0 and 4.04). "Cost increased 100 >>> 150", then "Cost decreased 150 >>>
  100". Two uses (the wiki), none free.
- **Team Deathmatch** (the wiki). Back 33 s after it's spent.

## 5. Design

### The rules

- **`AbilityConfig`** makes Shrouded Step played: its cost, confirmed now (Patch 4.04), its weapon
  and its line on Omen's card, and `ShroudedStep` (the unequip, the range, the drop, how high it can
  put him, the windup, his time gone and the server's grace), each with its status and source.
  `unequip` gives the gun back after the windup, his time gone and the draw.
- **`WeaponConfig`** adds `stepmark`, its mark in hand, 33rd of the weapons' wire ids.
- **`StepSim`** is new: where it takes him (`place`), whether he fits there (`fits`), whether he'd
  be in sight there (`sees`), and where the aim ends (`ends`).
- **`AbilitySim`** plays it as it plays the other orbs: C puts it in hand, and fire once it's ready
  is a `step` cast. The kit keeps when its windup ends and where it takes him (`shroud`, `stepAt`,
  `stepTo`), and each tick of it (`stepThrough`) tells of his going (`vanish`) and his arrival
  (`arrive`); `stepping`, `shrouding` and `shrouded` say how far along it is, and `busy` counts it.
- **`PlayerSim`** finds where its mark shows as it's fired, holds him through the windup and his
  time gone, puts him on the floor it marked as he goes, and frees him as he's there;
  **`PlayerCodec`** carries `stepAt` and `stepTo` in the ack.
- **`SoundConfig`** and **`FogOfWar`** add its sounds and its arrival's own curve, heard out to
  12.5 m (`CastCurves`, `Edges.arrive`).

### The server

- **`StepServer`** is new. As he goes it takes him out of play (`CombatServer.withdraw`) and tells
  every client, with where he's going; as he's there it puts him back (`CombatServer.reappear`, new:
  his health as it was); and if he's been gone longer than his time and the grace, it puts him back
  where he is. Where his last step takes him is his `VFLastStep` attribute.
- **`AbilityServer`** leaves his going to `StepServer`, and tells of his arrival only as far as it's
  heard; **`PlayerSimServer`** starts the snapshots over from where he's going, as it does at Run it
  Back's mark, so he's never shown, or hit, on the way. The 0.5 s he's gone is longer than the
  server's 140 ms rewind, so no shot rewound to where he was can hit him as he arrives.

### The client

- **`StepView`** is new: the targeter, others' windups, goings, shadows where they're going and
  arrivals, and your own screen through it. Its ring and shadow are adornments drawn over
  everything.
- **`PlayerController`** puts your view where you're going at once as you go, and leaves the blend
  out of a correction while you're gone; **`AbilityHud`** lights C with `STEP` under it through it;
  **`MinimapView`** draws where it would take you.
- **`AbilityEffects`** plays its sounds as it plays any cast, and **`WeaponLooks`**,
  **`GameSounds`** and **`SoundConfig`** give its mark in hand its look and the sounds, its arrival
  on its own curve.

### Not here yet

- **Its voice lines.** Nothing's said when there's nowhere to step.
- **Cut short.** Nothing here suppresses or captures, so only a death stops it in the windup.
- **The growl.** Nothing of him is heard through the windup but its first sound.
- **Enemy audio.** Patch 13.00 doesn't say how enemies' sounds of it changed, so ours are the same
  for everyone.
- **Spectating.** Whoever watches him isn't shown his screen's dark while he's gone.
- **From the Shadows.** His ultimate is on his card and the bar but not played yet.

## 7. Debug tools

- A player's `VFLastStep` is where their last step takes them, set as it's fired: the floor, in
  metres.
- `VFKit` reads `C2/2` with both charges, ends in `equip` while its mark's in hand, and in `step`
  through the windup and `shroud` while he's gone.
- `Workspace.VFStep` holds the targeter, a `StepMark` part with its `Ring` and `Ghost` adornments;
  others' windups as `Swirl` balls, their going as a `Puff`, the floor they're going to as a
  `Shadow` model with their entity id as its `Entity` attribute, and their arrival as a `Form`.
  PlayerGui's `VFStep` has your screen through it: the `Edge` frame's stroke through the windup, and
  `Dark` while you're gone.
- The minimap's icons include `Step` where it would take you.
- `VFTestButtons` 4096 presses C, 128 the left button, 1 W and 64 Space, so it can be readied and
  fired, and moving through it tried, without a key or a mouse button; `VFTestYaw` and `VFTestPitch`
  aim.

## 8. Refinement loop

1. Step through clips of a step from his going to his arrival, and set `away`.
2. Note where he stands after steps aimed at walls, and change how far off a wall it puts him.
3. Step under ledges and onto boxes of known height until one fails, and over walls from close to
   them, and change the room he needs, `above` and the line of sight.
4. Walk away from where he arrives until it isn't heard, and change the arrival's curve.
5. Re-run `luau tests/run.luau`.
