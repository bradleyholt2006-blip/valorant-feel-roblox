# System 45: Sova's Shock Bolt

Sova's Shock Bolt is his basic damage ability, and the second of his kit played. Q puts his bow in
hand with a shock bolt, ready in 0.8 s, and it's drawn, given bounces and loosed as the Recon Bolt
is: fire held draws it, a bar each third of a second, three at the most, alt fire adds a bounce, two
at the most, and fire let go looses it, faster and flatter the more bars were filled. It flies as
the Recon Bolt does and goes off whatever it meets while it has bounces left; with none left, where
it lands it bursts. Everyone near the burst that it has in sight takes its damage: 75 close in, less
further out, and none past 5 m. He has two, at 150 credits each. This system adds the Shock Bolt as
Sova's Q, its bow, the bolt's flight and burst, its damage to his enemies, his side and himself,
credited to it in the kill feed, and what everyone sees and hears of it. His Owl Drone and Hunter's
Fury aren't played yet.

As before, everything comes from public sources: Riot's page for Sova and three of its patch notes
and, for what Riot doesn't give, the VALORANT Wiki's pages on Sova, on projectiles and on Team
Deathmatch (read for system 44), nine guides (esportsdriven.com's, valoranttracker.org's,
THESPIKE.GG's, Boosting Ground's, esports.net's, Hotspawn's, dodge.gg's, Codashop's and Rankforge's)
and ONE Esports' report on Patch 4.08. The wiki's own page on the Shock Bolt couldn't be read: its
site refused every fetch, so whatever it gives of its radii isn't here. No game files, memory or
network traffic were used, and nothing was read from the live game. Here the wiki's numbers are
labelled community. Riot gives the bow, the explosive bolt detonating on collision and damaging
players nearby, held fire extending its range, alt fire's two bounces, its cost of 150, its most
damage, 75, its damage scaled out from there, and its damaging Fade's Prowler; the wiki gives its
two uses, its damage running from 1 at its outer radius to 75 at its inner, its projectile classes,
the Recon Bolt's, and its cooldown in Team Deathmatch; the guides give its 5 m radius, its damage
falling off from the centre, and its hurting teammates. Its inner radius, the shape of its fall-off,
whether walls stop it, how much its owner and his side take of it, its look and its sounds are ours.
Nothing in this system is measured. Status labels:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | Stated by the game's maker (Riot's patch notes and agent page) |
| **Community** | Published by a guide, a wiki or the press, not by the maker (the VALORANT Wiki, run by Weird Gloop) |
| **Estimate** | Our own number or reading. It must be measured and tuned before we trust it |

No source we could read gives its inner radius, how its damage falls off between its radii, whether
it must see those it hurts, how much its owner and his side take of it, how long after landing it
bursts, where it bursts from, or how far it's heard. Ours are in section 4: 1 m; a straight line
from 75 at 1 m to 1 at 5 m; a burst that sees each one's head, body or legs, as anyone's eye does,
from 0.1 m off the surface it met, the level and walls of ice in the way but not clouds or walls of
fire; all of it to its owner and his side, as to his enemies; at once as it lands; and heard out to
30 m, as the other abilities are.

## 1. Observable behaviour

- **In hand** (Riot's page for Sova). "EQUIP a bow with a shock bolt. FIRE to send the explosive
  bolt forward, detonating upon collision" "and damaging players nearby. HOLD FIRE to extend the
  range of the projectile." "ALT FIRE to add up to two bounces to this arrow." Nothing public we
  could read gives its own equip and unequip times; the wiki gives the Recon Bolt's
  (44-recon-bolt.md).
- **Its flight** (the wiki's page on projectiles). "The trajectory of a projectile is dependent on
  its speed and the scale at which it is affected by gravity." In our words: the Shock Bolt is in
  the same four classes as the Recon Bolt, with no bar, one, two and fully drawn, with a "+0.3 m
  offset" for the first two. dodge.gg: "tap for short range, full draw for maximum distance."
- **Its bounces** (Riot's page, Hotspawn and dodge.gg). Riot: "ALT FIRE to add up to two bounces to
  this arrow." Hotspawn: "Shock Bolts can come from cover with a strong charged two bounce".
  dodge.gg: "it can bounce off walls up to two times."
- **Its burst** (Riot's page, esports.net and Rankforge). Riot: "detonating upon collision" "and
  damaging players nearby." esports.net: "Upon collision, his Shock Bolt damages all players that
  are nearby." Rankforge: "Shock Dart deals area damage after landing."
- **Its damage** (Patch 4.08, ONE Esports, the wiki, THESPIKE.GG, Boosting Ground and esports.net).
  Patch 4.08: "Max Damage decreased 90 >>> 75" and "Radial damage has been scaled in accordance with
  new max damage.", as "Shock Dart has also taken a hit as we continue to sharpen damage outputs on
  non-ultimate abilities," "so that they kill less frequently". ONE Esports: "You now need to land
  two direct Shock Bolts in order to achieve the maximum damage of 150 and take an enemy down." In
  our words: the wiki's page for Sova has its damage run from 1 at its outer radius to 75 at its
  inner. THESPIKE.GG: "Damage: 1–75". Boosting Ground: "a maximum of 75 damage at the epicenter of
  the explosion." and "The damage decreases radially as the distance from the center increases."
  esports.net: "Players in the centre take the most damage."
- **Its radius** (esportsdriven.com and valoranttracker.org). esportsdriven.com: "it deals damage
  within a radius of 5 meters". In our words: valoranttracker.org's table gives its radius as 5m
  too. dodge.gg: "dealing damage in a small radius."
- **His side** (valoranttracker.org). Among its notes: "Damage teammates." Nothing we could read
  says how much, or whether it hurts Sova himself.
- **What else it damages** (Patch 5.12 and Rankforge). Patch 5.12, under its damage interaction
  updates: "Shock Bolt now damages:", Fade's Prowler, so that "abilities are interacting in a
  consistent manner across the board." Rankforge counts "Destroying enemy utility" among its uses.
- **Its cost and uses** (Patch 3.0, the wiki and the guides). Patch 3.0: Shock Dart (Q) "Cost
  increased 100 >>> 150". In our words: the wiki's page for Sova has it cost 150 for each of two
  uses. THESPIKE.GG: "Cost: 150 creds" and "Max charges: 2". Boosting Ground: "costing 150 Credits
  per charge." and "a maximum of two charges available." Codashop: "Each dart costs 150 credits."
- **Team Deathmatch** (the wiki's page on it). In our words: a Shock Bolt comes back 26 s after it's
  used there.

## 5. Design

### The rules

- **`AbilityConfig`** makes the Shock Bolt played (its weapon and its line on his card), and adds
  `ShockBolt` (the unequip, its most and least damage, its inner and outer radii, where it bursts
  from, and the shares its owner and his side take), each with its status and source. `unequip`
  gives the gun back 0.7 s after the loose.
- **`WeaponConfig`** adds `shockbow`, the bow with a shock bolt, 36th of the weapons' wire ids,
  firing nothing; its name, Shock Bolt, is what the kill feed shows.
- **`AbilitySim`** plays it as the Recon Bolt's bow (`bowed`): Q puts it in hand with nothing drawn
  and no bounces, `bow` looses a `bolt` cast of the Shock Bolt's, and Q while it's drawn lets the
  draw go. **`PlayerSim`** calls `bow` for either bow.
- **`ShockSim`** is new: its damage at each distance (`damageAt`), where its burst looks from
  (`eye`) and whom it hurts and how much (`hits`).
- **`SoundConfig`** adds its burst.

### The server

- **`ShockServer`** is new. It flies each bolt loosed at once through `BoltSim`, as nothing changes
  its path, and tells every client its path, how long after the loose it bursts, where, and the way
  the surface there faces (`flight`); bursts it on the server's clock, each one `ShockSim.hits`
  finds taking their share through **`CombatServer`**'s new `blast`, credited to its owner as the
  Shock Bolt; sets `VFLastShock` and `VFShockHits`; and forgets every bolt with a new round
  (`clear`, from **`Arena`**).
- **`ReconServer`** takes the Recon Bolt's casts alone; **`init.server`** starts and steps
  `ShockServer`.

### The client

- **`ShockView`** is new: your own bolts flown at once as you loose them, through `BoltSim`, and
  others' from the server; each bolt in the air and its burst as the frame on your screen reaches
  them, with their sounds.
- **`ReconView`** shows the Shock Bolt's draw under the crosshair as the Recon Bolt's, and draws
  recon bolts alone; **`BuyMenu`** adds its `Burst` row; **`WeaponLooks`** gives its bow a pale
  yellow bolt; **`init.client`** starts the view.

### Not here yet

- **His other abilities.** The Owl Drone and Hunter's Fury read NOT YET.
- **Utility.** It hurts players and the dummies alone: no stuck recon bolt, wall of ice or other
  ability takes anything from it, where Patch 5.12 has it damage Fade's Prowler and Rankforge has it
  destroy enemy utility.
- **Its own page's numbers.** Its inner radius and fall-off are ours, as the wiki's page on the
  Shock Bolt couldn't be read.
- **Through walls.** It hurts nobody out of its sight; no source we could read says whether walls
  stop it.
- **Meeting a player.** It flies through players and dummies and bursts only where it meets the
  level.
- **The 0.3 m offset** (the wiki's page on projectiles). It leaves from the eye.
- **Walls and clouds in flight.** Its flight meets the level alone, so it flies through walls of
  ice, walls of fire and the clouds.
- **The buy phase.** It can be loosed in the buy phase, as the other throws here can.
- **Sounds of its own.** Its bow, its draw, its loose and its flight sound as the Recon Bolt's.

## 7. Debug tools

- A player's `VFLastShock` is where their last bolt burst, in metres, set as it bursts, and
  `VFShockHits` whom it hurt and how much, each a name and its damage, in order of their names and
  joined by commas (`Dummy 3 (crouch) 48`), or nothing.
- `VFKit` reads `C0/1 Q2/2 E1/1 X0/8` on the course, and ends in `equip` while the bow's in hand,
  with `draw` and the bars while it's drawn and `bounce` and the bounces once any are added.
- `Workspace.VFShock` holds each bolt in the air as a `Bolt` part and each burst as a `Burst` ball
  while it flashes; its `Bolts` attribute counts the bolts in the air, and `Bursts` the bursts
  shown.
- PlayerGui's `VFRecon` shows its draw as the Recon Bolt's: `Bow`, `Bars` and `Bounces`.
- `VFTestButtons` 8192 presses Q, 128 the left button and 256 the right, so it can be drawn, given
  bounces, let go and loosed without a key or a mouse button; `VFTestYaw`, `VFTestPitch` and
  `VFTestAimPoint` aim.

## 8. Refinement loop

1. Land bolts a measured distance from a second player, further each time, and change `inner`,
   `outer` and `least`, and the line between them, until each takes what the reference's does.
2. Land them behind cover from a second player, and change who's in reach.
3. Land them at your own feet and an ally's, and change `selfShare` and `allyShare`.
4. Step through its landing and its burst, and change when it bursts.
5. Re-run `luau tests/run.luau`.
