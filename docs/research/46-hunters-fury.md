# System 46: Sova's Hunter's Fury

Sova's Hunter's Fury is his ultimate, and the third of his kit played. Once its 8 points are full, X
puts its bow in hand with three energy blasts, and its 6 s run from once it's drawn. Fire charges a
blast: through its 1 s windup he's rooted where he stands, his view turning only slowly, and as it
ends the blast goes from his eye along the crosshair, 66 m, through walls and everything else in its
way, and knocks him 1.5 m back. Every enemy within 2 m of its line, ahead of him, takes 80, however
far away, and is revealed to his side for 1 s. The blasts come no closer together than 2.125 s, and
it's over once its time's up or anything else is drawn. This system adds Hunter's Fury as Sova's X,
its bow, its blasts and what they do to his enemies, credited to it in the kill feed, his knockback,
the line on his minimap, and what everyone sees and hears of it. His Owl Drone isn't played yet.

As before, everything comes from public sources: seven of Riot's patch notes and, for what Riot
doesn't give, the VALORANT Wiki's pages on Sova and on Team Deathmatch (read for system 44), and the
guides of twelve sites (esportsdriven.com's, EarlyGame's, esports.net's, valoranttracker.org's,
Dignitas', Mobalytics' two, THESPIKE.GG's, Boosting Ground's, Sportskeeda's, dodge.gg's, iBUYPOWER's
and Aim Lab's). Riot's page for Sova doesn't describe Hunter's Fury, and the wiki's own page on it
couldn't be read: its site refused every fetch, so whatever it gives of its width, its windup or its
equip isn't here. No game files, memory or network traffic were used, and nothing was read from the
live game. Here the wiki's numbers are labelled community, and so is Riot's text for the ability,
which we could read only as THESPIKE.GG and the wiki quote it. Riot gives its 8 points, its cancel
by drawing anything else, its foe colouring, its ammo shown, what else it damages, its blind and
none behind him; the wiki gives its 6 s, its 80 a blast and its 1 s reveal, and its points in Team
Deathmatch; the guides give its windup, its rate, its three blasts, its range and radius, its
knockback, its rooting him, its piercing walls and the line on the minimap. Its equip, how slowly
his view turns, how long the knockback takes, whether it reaches his allies, its look and its sounds
are ours. Nothing in this system is measured. Status labels:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | Stated by the game's maker (Riot's patch notes) |
| **Community** | Published by a guide, a wiki or the press, not by the maker (the VALORANT Wiki, run by Weird Gloop) |
| **Estimate** | Our own number or reading. It must be measured and tuned before we trust it |

No source we could read gives how long its bow takes to draw, how slowly his view turns while a
blast charges, how long his knockback takes or which way it pushes him, whether it reaches his
allies, how near a body it must come, what happens to a blast charging as its time runs out, or what
it does in the buy phase. Ours are in section 4: 0.5 s, the gap between the 6.5 s three guides give
it and the 6 s esportsdriven.com gives without its equip; 0.15 of its usual speed; 0.25 s, level and
back from the way he faces; not his allies; within 2 m of the upright line from their feet to the
top of their head, and the half width of their body; the blast goes with the bow; and nobody caught
while the spawns' barriers are up.

## 1. Observable behaviour

- **In hand** (Riot's text, THESPIKE.GG, esportsdriven.com and Patch 4.08). Riot's text, as
  THESPIKE.GG gives it: "EQUIP a bow with three long-range wall-piercing energy blasts."
  esportsdriven.com: "Upon activation, he winds up and equips himself with a bow that holds multiple
  charges." Patch 4.08: "Hunter's Fury will now show how much ammo they have left when equipped".
  Nothing public we could read gives its equip time.
- **A blast** (Riot's text, valoranttracker.org, iBUYPOWER and Aim Lab). Riot's text: "FIRE to
  release an energy blast in a line in front of Sova," "dealing damage and revealing the location of
  enemies caught in the line." valoranttracker.org: "LEFT CLICK to shoot the energy blasts in the
  straight line in the direction Sova is looking at." iBUYPOWER: "When activated, Sova releases an
  energy blast in a straight line before him". Aim Lab: "Fire to release an energy blast in a line
  in front of Sova".
- **Through walls** (EarlyGame, esports.net, Mobalytics, THESPIKE.GG and iBUYPOWER). EarlyGame: "The
  arrows can be shot through walls". esports.net: "Sova equips a bow with three energy blasts that
  can pierce through walls." and "Since each blast is able to pierce more than one enemy".
  Mobalytics: "Fire up to three global arrows that pierce through terrain in a straight line."
  THESPIKE.GG: "Yes, Hunter's Fury fires through walls, but you've still gotta get the shot height
  right." iBUYPOWER: "Hunter's Fury can penetrate walls".
- **Its windup** (esportsdriven.com, esports.net, Dignitas, Mobalytics and dodge.gg).
  esportsdriven.com: "Windup time per charge: 1 second". esports.net: "once you start charging a
  blast Sova stays stationary." Dignitas: "You are free to move your bow around in Hunter's Fury.",
  but from "the moment you start to charge up a shot" "you can only move your crosshair around ever
  so slightly". Mobalytics: "it does have a slight delay so it can be somewhat easy to dodge if an
  enemy is prepared." dodge.gg: enemies "can reposition between blasts."
- **Its reach** (esportsdriven.com, EarlyGame, esports.net, valoranttracker.org, dodge.gg and
  Patch 13.09). esportsdriven.com: "The dimensions of this beam are 66 meters in length and a
  2-meter radius." EarlyGame: "2 by 66-meter range". esports.net: "Range: 66m". dodge.gg: "three
  long-range energy blasts that fire in a line across the entire map". Patch 13.09: "Fixed an issue
  where Sova's Hunter's Fury could affect players positioned behind him".
- **Its damage** (the wiki, esportsdriven.com, EarlyGame, Boosting Ground and esports.net). In our
  words: the wiki's page for Sova has each blast deal 80. esportsdriven.com: "Sova can deal 80
  damage in a single charge to all opponents that fall under the beam." Boosting Ground: "Each blast
  deals 80 damage." and "There is no damage falloff based on distance." esports.net: "Two hits onto
  the enemy guarantees a kill."
- **Its reveal** (Riot's text, the wiki and the guides). Riot's text: "revealing the location of
  enemies caught in the line". In our words: the wiki has it a real-time reveal of 1 s.
  esportsdriven.com: "Reveal duration: 1 second". esports.net: "Reveal duration on hit: 1.2s".
  valoranttracker.org: "Reveal Duration | 1.2 seconds (to self only)". Dignitas: "it will light them
  up, letting Sova know where they are before taking the next shot".
- **His knockback** (THESPIKE.GG, esportsdriven.com and EarlyGame). THESPIKE.GG and
  esportsdriven.com: "Self-knockback distance: 1.5 meters". EarlyGame: "a ranged AOE volley of three
  arrows, that damage, knockback, and reveal enemies".
- **Its blasts and its time** (Riot's text, THESPIKE.GG, esportsdriven.com and three more guides).
  Riot's text: "This ability can be RE-USED up to two more times while the ability timer is active."
  THESPIKE.GG: "Ammo: 3 charges" and "Duration: 6 seconds". esportsdriven.com: "Fire rate: 1 every
  2.125 seconds" and "Max duration: 6 seconds (Not including equip time)". esports.net: "Duration:
  6.5s". Boosting Ground: "The ability duration is 6.5 seconds." Sportskeeda: "Hunter's Fury, Sova's
  ultimate, can last a total of 6.5 seconds." dodge.gg: "Each blast has a brief delay between
  shots".
- **Ending it** (Patch 3.0 and esportsdriven.com). Patch 3.0: "Fixed a bug where cancelling Sova's
  Hunter's Fury while charging the shot allows player to skip" "unequip animation by equipping a
  weapon, ability, or Spike". esportsdriven.com: "At any second, the Owl can cancel the actions of
  Hunter's Fury".
- **Its look** (Patch 0.50 and Patch 9.04). Patch 0.50: "Added foe coloring for Sova's Hunter's
  Fury". Patch 9.04, among its known issues: "Sova's Hunter's Fury does not always play the minimum
  blind effect on opponents when the damage lands."
- **The minimap** (Dignitas, Mobalytics and THESPIKE.GG). Dignitas: its line there shows "the exact
  area-of-effect your shots will cover". Mobalytics: "You can see the path of the blast fire across
  the minimap to help you line up to certain areas." THESPIKE.GG: "Keep your eye on the minimap when
  you're using Sova's Ult, as it will show a range indicator".
- **What else it damages** (Patch 5.12 and Patch 6.0). Patch 5.12: "Hunters Fury now damages:",
  Raze's Satchel, Reyna's Leer, Fade's Prowler and Chamber's Rendezvous. Patch 6.0: "Fixed Sova's
  Hunter's Fury (X) and Breach's Aftershock (C) not damaging Raze's Blast Pack (Q)." and "Fixed Sova
  Hunter's Fury (X) damaging allied Fade's Haunt (E)."
- **Its points** (Patch 3.0, the wiki and the guides). Patch 3.0: "Ultimate points required
  increased 7 >>> 8". esportsdriven.com: "Ult Points: 8". Boosting Ground: "Hunter's Fury costs 8
  Ultimate Points." In our words: the wiki's page on Team Deathmatch has it need 70 points there.

## 5. Design

### The rules

- **`AbilityConfig`** makes Hunter's Fury played (its weapon and its line on his card), and adds
  `HuntersFury` (the equip, its time, a blast's windup and how fast the view turns through it, its
  damage, its range and radius, the reveal, and the knockback's distance and time), each with its
  status and source.
- **`WeaponConfig`** adds `furybow`, its bow, 37th of the weapons' wire ids: a click a blast, one
  every 2.125 s at the most, three of them and none to reload; its name, Hunter's Fury, is what the
  kill feed shows.
- **`AbilitySim`** casts it (a `fury` cast) and keeps its time (`furyUntil`), a blast's windup
  (`charge` and `charging`, a `charge` cast), the blast as the windup ends (a `blast` cast, from the
  eye along the crosshair), the knockback (`kicked` and `kickVelocity`) and its end (`calm`);
  putting an ability in hand ends it. **`PlayerSim`** arms its bow, turns its fire into a windup,
  roots him through it, and ends it once its time's up, its bow's gone or anything else is drawn, or
  as a plant or a defuse starts.
- **`FurySim`** is new: whom a blast catches (`target`, `reach` and `caught`).
- **`PlayerCodec`** carries the kit's four new numbers, and **`PlayerController`** checks them
  against the server's; **`SoundConfig`** adds its cast, its charge and its blast.

### The server

- **`FuryServer`** is new. It judges each blast as its owner's tick sends it, on the server's clock
  and with no rewind: whom it catches, each taking its damage through **`CombatServer`**'s `blast`,
  credited to its owner as Hunter's Fury, and each left alive revealed to its owner's side through
  **`ReconServer`**'s new `reveal`, so their minimap shows them and the fog of war sends them
  (**`MinimapServer`**); nobody while the spawns' barriers are up. It tells every client of the
  blast, and its owner's side whom it revealed and where they stood, and sets `VFLastFury` and
  `VFFuryHits`.
- **`AbilityServer`** leaves the blasts to it; **`init.server`** starts it.

### The client

- **`FuryView`** is new: its time and a blast's windup under the crosshair, your view slowed through
  the windup (**`CameraController`**'s new `turnScale`), others' blasts charging, each blast as the
  frame on your screen reaches it (yours at once), whom they reveal (**`ReconView`**'s new
  `reveal`), and the flash of being caught by one.
- **`MinimapView`** draws where a blast would go (`FuryView.aimed`); **`AbilityHud`** shows EQUIP
  and then its seconds left on X's square; **`WeaponLooks`** gives its bow a bolt of bright cyan;
  **`AbilityEffects`** sounds its casts; **`init.client`** starts the view.

### Not here yet

- **His Owl Drone.** It reads NOT YET.
- **Utility.** Its blasts hurt players and the dummies alone: no stuck recon bolt, wall of ice or
  other ability takes anything from them, where Patches 5.12 and 6.0 have them damage Raze's Blast
  Pack, Reyna's Leer, Fade's Prowler and Chamber's Rendezvous.
- **Its own page's numbers.** Its equip, its width either side of a body and how slowly the view
  turns are ours, as the wiki's page on Hunter's Fury couldn't be read.
- **The blind.** Being caught flashes your screen white for a moment, ours: nothing we could read
  says how strong or how long Patch 9.04's "minimum blind effect" is.
- **Rewind.** Each blast is judged where the server has everyone as it goes, not where its owner's
  screen showed them.
- **Enemies knocked back.** Only Sova is, by his own blasts.
- **The bow's motion.** It's held still: no string drawn back through a windup and no loose, and
  your blasts are drawn from just under your eye.

## 7. Debug tools

- A player's `VFLastFury` is where their last blast went from, in metres, set as it goes, and
  `VFFuryHits` whom it caught, their names in order and joined by commas
  (`Dummy 1 (static),Dummy 4 (static)`), or nothing.
- `VFKit` reads `C0/1 Q2/2 E1/1 X8/8` on the course, and ends in `fury` while the bow's in hand,
  with `charge` while a blast charges.
- PlayerGui's `VFFury` carries `Bow` (whether the bow's in hand), `Left` (the seconds it has left),
  `Charging` (whether a blast charges), `Turn` (how fast the view turns, a share of its usual) and
  `Blasts` (the blasts drawn); `Workspace.VFFury` holds each blast as a `Blast` part while it fades,
  and each blast of someone else's charging as a `Charge` ball.
- Whom a blast reveals shows as the Recon Bolt's do: `VFRecon`'s `Revealed`, and `Reveal_` and the
  entity's id in `Workspace.VFRecon`. The minimap's `Fury` icon is where a blast would go.
- `VFTestButtons` 32768 presses X, and 128 the left button, so it can be cast and fired without a
  key or a mouse button; `VFTestYaw`, `VFTestPitch`, `VFTestAimPoint` and `VFTestAimEntity` aim, and
  in Studio `VFTestGive` set to `ultimate` fills the ultimate's points.

## 8. Refinement loop

1. Stand a second player further off a blast's line each time, and change `radius` and
   `FurySim.WIDTH` until it catches them where the reference's does.
2. Step through clips of the cast, a windup and the blasts, and change `equip`, `windup`, `duration`
   and the bow's `fireRate`.
3. Measure where a blast leaves him and how fast, and change `kick` and `kickTime`.
4. Turn the mouse a set amount through a windup, and change `turn`.
5. Re-run `luau tests/run.luau`.
