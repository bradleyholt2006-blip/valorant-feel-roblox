# System 49: Ultimate Orbs in the spike rounds

The reference's maps have two Ultimate Orbs each (Fracture four), standing at fixed places on the
way to the sites, and both are up as every round starts. Holding the interact key at one takes it a
few seconds later, for a point towards the ultimate, and letting go first gets nothing. This system
puts two on each of our spike maps, where Team Deathmatch has its own (system 28): one in each
site's main (on Foundry, A long), both up as each round starts. Holding F on the ground within 1.5 m
of one that's up takes it in 2 s, standing rooted with the gun away, as through a defuse. It's over
with nothing gained the moment F is let go or the orb's gone, someone else having taken it. Taken,
an orb is gone until the next round, and its taker has a point of the ultimate. F defuses the spike
too now, and a defuse in reach goes before an orb. The taking is stepped in each player's simulation
beside the plant and the defuse, so the client predicts it as it does them. A gold bar shows it, a
prompt asks for F at one, its start is a whoosh that enemies hear as far as a plant, a chime marks
one taken, and the minimap shows the orbs up as gold dots.

As before, everything comes from public sources: Riot's patch notes (0.49, 1.02, 5.01, 8.0 and
11.09) and, for what Riot doesn't give, guides (valorantinfo.gg, Switchblade Gaming, Mobalytics,
Sportskeeda, Dexerto, gamepressure and keymap.io, with Gfinity's read for system 17), Sirus Gaming's
report of a 2021 glitch, and two threads on VLR.gg. No game files, memory or network traffic were
used, and nothing was read from the live game. Riot gives the count, a pickup that can be cancelled
and the short cue enemies hear; the community gives the rest, and nobody publishes how long a pickup
takes. Nothing in this system is measured. Status labels:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | Stated by the game's maker (Riot's patch notes) |
| **Community** | Published by a guide, a wiki, the press or players, not by the maker |
| **Estimate** | Our own number or reading. It must be measured and tuned before we trust it |

No source we could read says how long the key must be held, how close to an orb one must stand,
whether the taker can move or shoot, what becomes of an orb two players finish taking at once, or
whether a full ultimate can take one. Ours are in section 4: 2 s; within 1.5 m across the floor;
rooted with the gun away, as through a defuse; the one the server steps first has it; and a full
ultimate takes it and loses its point.

## 1. Observable behaviour

- **Two a map** (Riot and the community). Patch 8.0 took Icebox's B orb away "to bring it more in
  line with our other maps (with Fracture being the exception with 4)". Mobalytics: "Every map also
  has two ultimate orbs that can be collected by agents." Switchblade Gaming: "Nearly every map
  places two orbs per round — one near A site, one near B."
- **Up every round** (the community). Sportskeeda: "One can collect Ultimate Orbs that spawn at
  specific locations on the map every round." Switchblade Gaming: "They spawn at fixed locations at
  round start and reset each round."
- **Where** (the community and Riot). valorantinfo.gg has them "usually near attacker side spawn and
  at key points"; Sportskeeda has Ascent's "in A main, and the other in B main", Split's "at A main
  and in B main 'garage.'" and Haven's "in A long and the other is in C long". Riot took Icebox's B
  orb away because it "was very farmable by Attackers".
- **Taking one** (the community and Riot). With the interact key, F: keymap.io's "Use object /
  interact" is "Defuse, pick up orbs, open doors". Dexerto, on Spike Rush's orbs, which "are
  acquired in the same way as an Ultimate orb": "you'll have to interact with one for a few seconds
  to pick up the effect". Switchblade Gaming calls stopping for one on the way "The two-second
  detour". Riot fixed "canceling a plant or orb pickup" playing "the wrong (slower) re-equip
  animation" (Patch 0.49): a pickup can be cancelled, as a plant can, and the gun's equipped again
  after it. Patch 1.02 fixed a bug "where you could get stuck grabbing orbs".
- **A point of the ultimate** (the community). Gfinity, read for system 17, has one Ultimate Point
  for "Collecting an Ultimate Orb", as for a kill; valorantinfo.gg: "each orb adds one ult point to
  the agent's ultimate charge."
- **Heard** (Riot and VLR.gg). Patch 5.01 gives KAY/O's revive "a short audio cue when the revive
  has started, matching a similar gameplay pattern to playing around ultimate orbs or tapping the
  bomb". Players listen for it: "if you hear someone getting ult orb", and "defenders everywhere to
  hear if they pick up the orb".
- **Seen** (VLR.gg, Riot and gamepressure). The scoreboard shows a player's points rise as one's
  taken: "you can see the ult orbs be updated in real time". Patch 11.09 updated the highlight of
  "World interactions including Switches, Ropes, Ziplines and Ultimate Orbs". In Spike Rush, "All
  powerup orbs are perfectly visible on the radar" (gamepressure).
- **A team down a player** (valorantinfo.gg). "If a team is down one or more players, their spawn
  will get one or two ult orbs added at the beginning of a round", and "These orbs disappear once
  the spike has been planted."
- **Worth the stop?** (VLR.gg). "it's not always a great idea to stop and get the orbs for an x
  amount of time", and "They cost time and also sometimes cost your position."

## 5. Design

### The rules

`UltimateOrbs` (new) keeps the numbers and the orbs as plain data, and `SpikeSim` steps the taking:

- **`UltimateOrbs.placesOf`** gives where each of a map's orbs stands, in metres: Team Deathmatch's
  `Pickups[layout].ultimate` past the map's origin, worked out once; none for the course. **`new`**,
  **`roundStarted`**, **`take`** and **`upOf`** keep which are up: all as a round starts, and one
  taken once. **`encode`** and **`decode`** turn which are up into Workspace's `VFOrbs` (`1,2`) and
  back. **`reaches`** says whether feet can take an orb, and **`inReach`** which of those up they'd
  take.
- **`SpikeSim`** has a third channel, `COLLECTING` (3), with the orb's number (`orb`). With nothing
  under way, on the ground and not held over, F starts it at the orb `inReach` gives among
  `Spike.Context.orbs`, once neither a plant nor a defuse has started: it roots, and holsters the
  weapon. Each tick after, it carries on while F's held and the orb's up and in reach, and otherwise
  ends with nothing and the weapon drawn again; at `collectTime` it ends with a `collected` act (the
  orb's number and where it stood), held over until F's let go, and the weapon drawn again.
  **`stowed`** (new) is whether the weapon's away: defusing, or taking an orb. A defuse starts on F
  as on 4.
- **`Spike.Context`** has `orbs`, the orbs up, by number; nil for none.
- **`MovementSim.Buttons`** has `Interact` (65536), F held.
- **`PlayerSim`** roots a taking as it does a plant or a defuse: nothing's cast, an ability in hand
  is put away, and nothing fires (`SpikeSim.stowed`). In a dash F's ignored, as 4 and fire are.
- **`PlayerCodec`**: the seventeenth button rides in the input's slot byte (64); the ack's spike
  flags carry the orb's number in their top five bits; a pose's channel 3 is a taking.

### The server

- **`OrbServer`** (new) builds the map's orbs in `Workspace.VFUltimateOrbs` as each round of the
  spike rounds starts, all up, and clears them once the spike rounds are over. Which are up goes out
  as Workspace's `VFOrbs` and into every player's simulation (`SpikeServer.setOrbs`). A `collected`
  act takes its orb if it's still up, and gives its taker the point (`AbilityServer.addPoints`).
- **`SpikeServer`** gives everyone's context the orbs up.
- **`Arena`** tells OrbServer as each round starts, after the new spike, and stops it with the spike
  rounds.
- **`PlayerSimServer`** sends a taker's pose with no weapon, as a defuser's.

### The client

- **`ClientSpike`** reads `VFOrbs` into the context the prediction uses, and gives the orbs up to
  the HUD and the minimap (`ClientSpike.orbs`).
- **`InputSampler`** sends F held as `Interact`. A press of F still takes a gun off the ground
  (system 15).
- **`SpikeHud`**: the prompt at an orb, in gold, and the bar.
- **`GameSounds`**: the whoosh, the player's own and others', and the chime.
- **`MinimapView`**: a gold dot for each orb up, named `Orb_<n>`, and its ScreenGui's `Orbs`
  attribute.
- **`WeaponEffects`** hides the viewmodel while taking one, **`DebugHud`**'s spike line reads
  `taking an orb`, and **`PlayerController`** reconciles when the server's orb differs from the
  prediction's.

### Not here yet

- **A team down a player.** Its spawn doesn't get one or two orbs more each round (valorantinfo.gg).
- **The time.** 2 s is ours, until clips give the reference's.
- **The scoreboard.** Ours has no column of ultimate points, so a point taken shows on the ability
  bar's pips and in `VFKit` alone.
- **Points across a match.** What a half, overtime and a death to the spike do to the points is for
  system 50.
- **The defuse prompt** still reads `HOLD 4 TO DEFUSE`, though F defuses too.
- **The fog.** It sends a taker to enemies within a plant's reach for the whole taking, where the
  reference has only a short cue as it starts.
- **The look and sound.** The reference's orb, its highlight, its sounds and its voice lines aren't
  copied.

## 7. Debug tools

- Workspace's `VFOrbs` is which orbs are up, by number (`1,2`), and `Workspace.VFUltimateOrbs` holds
  each orb (`UltimateOrb1`) and its mark (`UltimateOrb1Mark`).
- `VFTestButtons` 65536 holds F.
- The F3 HUD's spike line reads `taking an orb` and how far along it is, and `VFKit`'s `X` part
  counts the point.
- The minimap's ScreenGui, `VFMinimap`, carries the orbs on it as `Orbs` (`1,2`), and their icons
  are `Orb_1` and `Orb_2`.
- `VFMatchSkip` skips the buy phase, so the orbs can be reached at once.

## 8. Refinement loop

1. Frame-count public clips of a pickup, and set `collectTime` to the reference's; do the same for
   how close one must stand.
2. Play the spike rounds, and watch how often players stop for an orb, and which side takes them
   more. If one side always does, move them.
3. Re-run `luau tests/run.luau`.
