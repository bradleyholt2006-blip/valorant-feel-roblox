# System 50: The ultimate's points across a match

In the reference, an agent's ultimate points carry from round to round, but not across the whole
match: switching sides wipes them, every overtime round puts everyone a few short of their ultimate
whatever they had, and Swiftplay starts each half with two. A death's point comes as the next round
starts, and only an enemy's kill earns one. Until now the spike rounds kept the points through the
half (system 17's estimate), gave a death's point the moment it happened, whatever caused it, and
had no rule for overtime or Swiftplay. This system sets the points as each round of the spike rounds
starts. The second half's first round wipes everyone's points, full or not. In Swiftplay, each half
starts everyone on 2 (3 for an ultimate of 9 points, which none of ours is). Every overtime round, a
sudden death's included, puts everyone 3 short of what their ultimate needs. Every other round pays
each player a point for each death in the last. And everywhere, a death no enemy caused, to the
spike's blast, a teammate's ability or one's own, earns nothing, nor does killing a teammate.

As before, everything comes from public sources: Riot's patch notes (1.0, 1.03, 5.12 and 10.10) and,
for what Riot doesn't give, guides (valorantinfo.gg, Mobalytics, Gfinity, Switchblade Gaming,
Turbosmurfs, ValBoosting, valorfeed.gg and The Blog Timer), the press (Dot Esports, Our Culture) and
a thread on VLR.gg. No game files, memory or network traffic were used, and nothing was read from
the live game. Riot gives Swiftplay's points, overtime's in 2020 and which deaths earn one; the
community gives the half and the death point's timing. Nothing in this system is measured. Status
labels:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | Stated by the game's maker (Riot's patch notes) |
| **Community** | Published by a guide, the press or players, not by the maker |
| **Estimate** | Our own number or reading. It must be measured and tuned before we trust it |

Riot's Patch 1.03 put everyone "4 points short of their ultimate" in each overtime round in 2020;
every later source we could read has three, and no patch note we found says when it changed. Ours is
three, until a clip of an overtime round settles it. No source we could read says whether the point
for a death in a half's last round survives the half's wipe, what Unrated's single Endgame round or
Swiftplay's sudden death does to the points, whether a death that Sage's Resurrection undoes still
earns its point, or whether killing a teammate earns the killer one. Ours, in section 4: it goes
with the wipe; each is an overtime round; yes; and no.

## 1. Observable behaviour

- **The half wipes them** (the community). valorantinfo.gg: "At the end of the first set of rounds,
  the ultimate abilities each agent has left will reset before switching sides." Mobalytics:
  "Ultimate points are wiped when teams switch sides, so be sure to use your ultimate before that
  happens."
- **Swiftplay's two** (Riot and the community). Patch 5.12, as Swiftplay came in: "2 Ult points
  granted at the start of each half". Patch 10.10, in 2025, on Tejo's ultimate: it "will start at 3
  points instead of 2 in Swiftplay", and "Ultimates at 9 points in the future will be automatically
  updated to scale." Our Culture, once the mode was permanent: "All players have 2 points for their
  ultimate each half."
- **Overtime** (Riot and the community). Patch 1.03: "Each round will have a full reset, setting all
  players at 5000 credits and 4 points short of their ultimate." valorantinfo.gg: "If the game goes
  into overtime, ultimate abilities will reset after every round." Later sources have three: Dot
  Esports, on a player's post in September 2023, has "all agents need just three points to charge up
  their ultimates in overtime"; Turbosmurfs, "ultimate points set three short of full"; and The Blog
  Timer, "Players get 5,000 credits and sit three points short of their ultimates."
- **A death's point** (the community). Gfinity: "Every death will get you one Ultimate Point."
  valorantinfo.gg: "Dying during a round grants one ult point at the beginning of the next round."
- **Only an enemy's** (Riot and the community). Patch 1.0, as the game launched: "Dying to a
  non-enemy player will no longer grant ultimate points", and "This includes dying to the spike,
  fall damage, a teammate's ability, or your own ability." It was to end "farming ultimate points"
  by "self-killing at the end of the pistol round, or team kill at the start of a save round". Asked
  since whether dying to the spike earns one, players on VLR.gg answer "you don't, it doesn't count
  as a death in your kda either", and that "players die to spike to deny the other team the orbs".
  ValBoosting: dying to the Spike "will not award ultimate points to the opposing team", and of a
  player who jumps to their death, "the player who jumps off the map does not seem to receive an
  ultimate orb themselves for the death."
- **The plant's and the defuse's** (the community), as before (system 17). valorantinfo.gg: "The
  agent that plants or diffuses the spike will earn an ult point."

## 5. Design

### The rules

- **`AbilityConfig.Points`** has `swiftplay` (2), `swiftplayNine` (3) and `overtimeShort` (3).
- **`AbilitySim.roundPoints`** (new) sets the points as a round of the spike rounds starts, after
  the kit's own start (`reset`, `newHalf` or `newRound`), by the round's kind
  (**`AbilitySim.RoundKind`**): a `match`'s or a `half`'s first starts them over at none, or at
  Swiftplay's; an `overtime` round puts them `overtimeShort` short of what the ultimate needs; and
  any other `round` pays what the player is owed for deaths in the last. `died` with no points adds
  none.
- **`MatchRules.roundKind`** (new) says which kind each round is: the first, the first of the second
  half, one past regulation (a sudden death's too), or any other.

### The server

- **`AbilityServer`** keeps each player's owed points: a death in the spike rounds adds its point
  there, if an enemy caused it, in place of on the kit; elsewhere a death no enemy caused adds none,
  and a teammate's death brings its killer nothing (**`CombatServer.allies`** says whether two
  players share a side). **`roundStarted`** now takes the round's kind and whether it's Swiftplay,
  runs the kit's start, then `roundPoints` with what's owed, and clears it. A kit started over for
  another agent keeps its points in the spike rounds.
- **`Arena`** passes each round's kind (`MatchRules.roundKind`) and whether the match is
  Swiftplay's.

### Not here yet

- **The record.** A death to the spike's blast still counts as a death on the scoreboard (system
  16), where a VLR.gg reply has it not counting.
- **A half defuse.** valorfeed.gg counts a half-defuse for the defuse's point; ours pays only a
  whole defuse (system 17).
- **Showing it.** Ours has no scoreboard column of ultimate points (system 49), so the wipe, the
  reset and a death's point show on the ability bar's pips and in `VFKit` alone.
- **Overtime's number.** 3 until a clip settles it against Riot's 4.

## 7. Debug tools

- A player's `VFKit` ends with the ultimate's points and what it needs, as `X2/8`.
- `VFTestGive` set to `ultimate` fills the points in Studio, to see the half or overtime take them.
- `VFMatchSkip` skips a phase, and `VFMatchFormat` picks the format (`swiftplay`, or `competitive`
  for its win-by-two overtime). Swiftplay reaches its half after four rounds and its sudden death
  after eight.

## 8. Refinement loop

1. Find a public clip of a competitive overtime round's buy phase with the scoreboard open, and set
   `overtimeShort` to how far short its players start.
2. Find a clip of a death with the player's own ultimate in view, and move the death's point back to
   the moment of death if it rises then.
3. Re-run `luau tests/run.luau`.

## Known gaps to check in Studio

- Swiftplay's 2 points at the start, kept through an agent picked in the first buy phase.
- A death's point paid as the next round starts, and a kill's at once.
- No point for a death to the spike's blast, to one's own Shock Bolt or to a teammate's, nor for the
  teammate.
- The half wiping a full ultimate, and a death in its last round earning nothing across it.
- A sudden death's start 3 short of full, whatever was had.
- The standard format: none at the start, none after the half, and 3 short in its sudden death.
