# System 75: Reyna's Empress

Reyna's Empress (X) can now be played, the last of her abilities. With its 7 points, X sends her
into a frenzy after a 1 s activation, her gun away meanwhile, and the frenzy lasts until she dies or
the round ends. Through it her rounds come 15% faster and her draws and reloads 25% faster, though
each spray climbs and settles as it would without it; each Soul Orb she earns heals her at once as
Devour does, the orb kept, and what a heal takes her past 100 stays; and Dismiss spends no charge
and hides her from her enemies. This system adds the cast and its frenzy, the quickened gun, Devour
cast for her and the heal that stays, a Dismiss unseen by players and bots, how she looks and sounds
in it, the wire, and how bots cast it and the rest of her kit through it. It also fixes what Leer's
run in Studio found (section 5).

As before, everything comes from public sources: Riot's patch notes 1.09, 2.03, 8.11, 9.05 and 12.02
and Riot's page on her (in Spanish), Liquipedia's page on Reyna, THESPIKE.GG's page on her, and
guides by esports.net, Mobalytics and gamepressure.com. No game files, memory or network traffic
were used, nothing was read from the live game, and no Riot asset or sound is used. Riot gives the
fire rate, that the frenzy lasts until she dies or the round ends, that a buff to the fire rate
leaves the recoil as at the gun's own rate, that heals in it stay, its points, and that Devour casts
itself in it without taking the orb. The guides give the activation, the draws and reloads, that
Dismiss hides her, and that her Soul Orb abilities' charges are endless in it. How she looks and
sounds in it, what Q does, what her side sees of her Dismiss, and what bots do are ours. Nothing in
this system is measured yet. Status labels:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | Stated by the game's maker (Riot's patch notes and its page on her) |
| **Community** | Published by a guide or a wiki, not by the maker (Liquipedia, THESPIKE.GG, esports.net, Mobalytics, gamepressure.com) |
| **Estimate** | Our own number or reading. It must be measured and tuned before we trust it |

## 1. Observable behaviour

- **The frenzy** (Liquipedia, THESPIKE.GG, esports.net). Liquipedia: "Instantly enter a frenzy,
  increasing firing, equip and reload speed by 25%." and "Gain infinite charges of Soul Harvest
  abilities." THESPIKE.GG: "Cast time: 1 second" and "Buff: +25% each to Equip speed, Reload speed,
  and Recovery speed. +15% to Fire rate". esports.net: "increased gun equip and reload speed by 25%,
  and increased gun fire speed by 15%."
- **Fire 15% faster** (Patch 1.09). "Fire rate bonus decreased from 25% >>> 15%", a "fire rate
  increase (matching Brimstone's stim below)". Liquipedia's 25% to firing is from before it.
- **Until she dies** (Patch 8.11, THESPIKE.GG). "Empress no longer has a timer and is permanent
  until Reyna is killed or the round ends.", one of the changes "Removing the time restrictions on
  her Devour (Q) and Empress (X)". Before it, as THESPIKE.GG has it: "Though its duration is limited
  to 30 seconds initially, getting a kill will renew that countdown."
- **The recoil** (Patch 9.05, Liquipedia). Under "Stim Behavior", Riot heard "that combat stim
  effects like Reyna's Empress can feel disruptive" when spraying, and so "updated all buffs that
  increase your rate of fire to have consistent recoil control", recoil "that matches the default
  rate of fire.", for "players who are stimmed to utilize their learned muscle memory". "This change
  only affects how the stims modify recoil without affecting the applied spread reduction."
  Liquipedia's history of her: "Increased rate of fire to have consistent recoil control that
  matches the default rate of fire".
- **Devour, cast for her** (Riot's page, Mobalytics, gamepressure.com). Riot's page, of Devour: "Si
  LA EMPERATRIZ está activa, lanzará automáticamente esta habilidad sin consumir el orbe." (with
  Empress on, it casts itself, without using up the orb). Mobalytics: "Devour will automatically
  cast when you get a kill." gamepressure.com: "she also gets an unlimited charges of Dismiss and
  Devour abilities."
- **Dismiss, unseen** (Liquipedia, THESPIKE.GG, Mobalytics). Liquipedia, of Dismiss: "If Empress is
  active, also become invisible." THESPIKE.GG: "and it enhances her Dismiss ability to turn Reyna
  invisible, too." Mobalytics: "intangible AND invisible".
- **Heals that stay** (Patch 12.02). Devour's "Overheal applied by Devour is now temporary and
  lasts 10s", but "Healing applied while in Empress is still permanent"; and Empress's "Ult points
  increased 6 >> 7".
- **Room for the 1v5** (Patch 2.03). Halving her Devour and Dismiss charges was to "limit how much
  impact she can have round over round when she's heavily out-gunning her opponents", leaving
  "enough room for the 1v5 dream when Empress(X) is active."
- **What she sees** (gamepressure.com, from 2020). "The vision is slightly darkened and all
  opponents are highlighted by a clear red border and a red glow,"

her past 100 stays; her side sees her Dismiss as ever; she glows violet; and there's no spread

## 5. Design

### The rules

- **`AbilityConfig`** adds Empress's activation, fire rate and handling, each with its status and
  source, and `EMPRESS_ATTRIBUTE`; Empress is playable.
- **`AbilitySim`** casts it (`empress`), spending the points, and keeps when its frenzy starts
  (`empressAt`): `crowning` through its activation, `frenzied` after, and `unseen` through a Dismiss
  in it. In the frenzy Q does nothing and Dismiss spends no charge, its cast `hidden`; a Devour cast
  for her is `auto`. `ultimateOn` and `busy` cover it, and the kit's summary reads `crown` and
  `frenzy`.
- **`WeaponSim`** quickens the gun by its `rate` (rounds) and `handling` (draws and reloads), which
  `PlayerSim` sets each tick from the kit; between taps its recovery counts from when the gun could
  have fired again at its own rate.
- **`PlayerSim`** puts the gun away through the activation, and **`PlayerCodec`** carries when the
  frenzy starts and the gun's quickening on the ack.

### The server

- **`SoulServer`** casts Devour for her in the frenzy as she earns each orb, its heal started and
  every client told (`AbilityServer.relay`), keeps what a heal finished in it takes her past 100,
  and sets her `VFEmpress` while she's in it.
- **`AbilityServer`** adds `relay`, telling clients of a cast the server made, and passes on
  `hidden` and `auto`.
- **`BotServer`** hides a Reyna in a Dismiss in her Empress's frenzy from bots' looks, and a bot
  playing her leaves Devour to its Empress. It and **`PlayerSimServer`** put the gun away through
  the activation in her pose.

### The client

- **`RemoteEntities`** doesn't draw a Reyna in an Empress Dismiss for her enemies, nor the name over
  her, and through the frenzy draws her with its glow (`BodyView`), by her `VFEmpress`.
- **`SoulView`** glows her screen's edges in the frenzy and offers Dismiss alone; **`AbilityHud`**
  reads `WINDUP`, `FRENZY`, `AUTO` and `FREE`; **`AbilityEffects`** plays the Devour cast for her;
  **`GameSounds`** holds the draw through the activation, and **`SoundConfig`** has the cast's
  sound.
- **`CombatHud`**'s reload and draw bars and **`WeaponEffects`**' draw and reload keep up with the
  quicker hands; the HUD's `Ammo` is the magazine shown, for scripted checks.

### Bots

- **`BotUtility`** casts it on the push (`push:empress`), holding a way in (`stall:empress`) and in
  the modes that respawn (`empress`), and lets the rest of the kit be cast through the frenzy.

### Fixes from Leer's run in Studio

Every check in section 78 passed, and so did the re-runs of sections 76 and 77. They found:

- **The eye in hand.** Its pupil was on its far side, never seen; it's now turned to her, as the eye
  cast turns its pupil to whoever watches it (`WeaponLooks`).
- **The checklist.** Leer's break is heard at the eye, not as yours; `VFLastLeered` is set a second
  after the eye's gone, as late shots still count till then; LR8 now fits Skirmish's two charges;
  bots' casts read `push:leer`, with no number, a retaking side's too; and in Basic Training the
  eye's left to go before Dummy 1 is downed, as it opens in front of its head and took the first
  shots.

### Not here yet

- **What she sees.** gamepressure.com has her view darkened and every enemy picked out in red; here
  her screen's edges glow, and nobody's picked out.
- **The spread reduction.** Patch 9.05 leaves stims' "applied spread reduction" as it was, and no
  source says how much it is; here her spread's as it would be without the frenzy.
- **Suppression.** Since Patch 5.09 a suppressed Reyna's kills don't heal her (Liquipedia); nothing
  here suppresses yet.
- **Voices.** The reference's Reyna calls out as it starts; there are no voices here.
- **The activation, the glow, the edges and the sound** are ours until clips are stepped through.

## 7. Debug tools

- Anyone's `VFEmpress`: true while they're in Empress's frenzy (a bot's on its stand-in).
- `VFKit` reads `crown` through its activation and `frenzy` through the frenzy, its points `X0/7`
  once it's cast.
- In Studio the server's Output has `[VF] soul: <name> empress devour, <n> kept` as each orb she
  earns in the frenzy heals her, and `[VF] soul: <name> overheal kept (empress)` as a heal finishes
  in it past 100.
- PlayerGui's `VFSouls` `Prompt` reads `E DISMISS` alone in the frenzy, with an orb in reach, and
  `VFCombatHud`'s `Ammo` is the magazine shown.

## 8. Refinement loop

1. Play her on the course and against bots: whether the activation's too long, whether the faster
   gun shows in a fight, whether the glow and the edges are too much or too little, and whether an
   unseen Dismiss is too strong.
2. Tune `AbilityConfig.Reyna`, `BodyView`'s glow and `SoulView`'s edges, and re-run
   `luau tests/run.luau`.
